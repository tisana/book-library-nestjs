## Actionable findings

1. **[P2] Preserve request-to-bucket identity in the trusted-proxy contract.** `test/production-bootstrap.e2e-spec.ts:723-732` projects away bucket identity and compares only sorted final counts. For the trusted fixture at lines 683-689, the correct identities are `[client-A, client-A, client-B, direct-peer, direct-peer]`, producing `[1,2,2]`. A wrong resolver that selects the rightmost private proxy instead produces `[proxy-A, proxy-B, proxy-A, direct-peer, direct-peer]`, also producing `[1,2,2]`. Thus the passing assertion does not prove that changing a spoofed leftmost address/private proxy preserves the first-untrusted client's bucket, or that another first-untrusted client creates a distinct bucket. This is a security regression blind spot in an explicit T2 requirement. Inspect opaque bucket keys/ids after each request and assert that requests one and two increment the same bucket, request three creates another bucket, and malformed/unsupported headers share the direct-peer bucket. Keep diagnostics free of raw identities and credentials. A pure grouping calculation independently confirmed the identical histograms; no production source mutation or suite rerun was needed.

2. **[P2] Redact safely across the diagnostic buffer cutoff.** `test/support/production-process.ts:144-146` slices the combined raw output to 64,000 characters before `redact()` at lines 102-117 matches configured secrets. When the cutoff falls inside a secret, only its suffix remains, so the exact-value replacement cannot recognize it and `diagnostics()` or the startup error can expose that credential fragment. For example, a synthetic secret followed by 63,990 padding characters retains the secret's final ten characters at the start of the buffer. A read-only synthetic calculation confirmed `fullSecretRetained=false` and `secretSuffixStillVisible=true`; no real credential was printed. Make retention/redaction preserve a safe overlap or pending segment across chunks and truncation, and expose only redacted complete output. Add a probe that forces the retention cutoff through a configured secret and verifies that fragments are absent, alongside the existing split-chunk probe. No leak is demonstrated in the recorded successful runs, but the reusable harness's redaction guarantee currently fails on this deterministic noisy-output path.
